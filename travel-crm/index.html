Two Agents, One Team

One works the phone, one works the chats. Neither of them freelances — anything sensitive goes to a human.

Aanya
Voice agent

Calls every new enquiry within minutes of it landing, while the traveller is still thinking about the trip.

  • Confirms the month, the number of travellers and the budget per person
  • Answers the same questions consultants answer forty times a week — visa rules, price-from, voucher status
  • Switches to Hindi or Hinglish the moment the caller does
  • Moves the enquiry to Qualified and promises a plan on WhatsApp
Wish
Auto-reply agent

Answers on WhatsApp and Instagram first, so nobody waits an hour for a reply to “is this still available?”

  • Replies autonomously when it is confident, drafts for a human when it isn't
  • Holds the thread until a consultant picks it up
  • Caps its own replies per thread so a human takes over naturally
  • Stays quiet outside working hours beyond a simple acknowledgement
Scoring
Background agent

Scores every open enquiry for conversion likelihood and says, in plain words, why.

  • Re-scores as the traveller engages
  • Surfaces the hot enquiries at the top of the day
  • Explains the score instead of just asserting it
Itinerary & follow-up
Draft-only agent

Builds a day-by-day plan from an Instagram reel and nudges threads that have gone quiet.

  • Rebuilds a reel's trip for the traveller's own dates, pace and budget
  • Leaves every itinerary in draft until a consultant approves it
  • Follows up on silent threads without being asked

The Guardrails Matter More Than the Agent

An agent that can quote a refund is a liability. These rails are enforced in the prompt and in the tool layer — the locked ones can't be switched off at all.

Never quotes refunds or cancellation feesAny cancellation, refund or dispute is acknowledged and handed to a consultant with an urgent callback task.
Never promises availability or a confirmed priceReplies say “from ₹X per person, subject to availability”. Confirmed numbers only ever come from a consultant-sent itinerary.
Escalates on cancellation, complaint, medical and visa rejectionHand-off topics tag the enquiry, flag it for attention and land it in the human review queue.
Passport numbers are masked everywhere the agent can seeSummaries, transcripts and agent events show ••••1234. Full numbers live only in the document vault.
Human review before any AI itinerary is sentAgent-built plans stay in draft until a consultant approves them.
Payment links only from consultantsThe chat agent never pastes a payment link — it tells the traveller a consultant will send one.

And the Rest of the Business

The agents are the new part. Underneath it is a travel CRM that runs the whole trip.

Instagram to Booking

A comment on a reel becomes a DM, a DM becomes a qualified enquiry, and the source stays attached to the lead all the way to the booking.

Itineraries From Reels

Paste the reel a traveller fell in love with and get that trip rebuilt for their dates, pace and budget — with transparent per-person pricing.

One Inbox

WhatsApp, Instagram and email in a single thread per traveller, with a reply clock on each one so nothing ages out quietly.

Every Call Scored

Agent calls and consultant calls in one log, with transcripts, outcomes, sentiment and a quality score — so coaching isn't guesswork.

Trips, Docs & Visas

Travellers, documents, visa status and vouchers tracked through departure, with passports held in a vault rather than a chat thread.

Payments & Collections

Advances, balances and overdue collections tracked against each trip, with supplier payables on the other side of the ledger.

Ask the Data

Ask a question about the business in plain English and get the answer back, the same way our sales CRM does it.

Full AI Audit

Every agent decision — call, reply, score, draft — logged with what it saw and what it did. You can always answer “why did it say that?”

Role-Based Access

Consultants, operations, finance and managers each get their own view, with approvals where money and documents are involved.
