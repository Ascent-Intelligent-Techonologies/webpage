How It Works

From first click to closed deal, without a single lead falling through the cracks.

Capture

Leads from Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, your website and walk-ins land on your board automatically — no manual entry.

Route & Call

New leads are auto-assigned round-robin across your team. Reps call straight from the CRM — every call recorded automatically.

Prioritize

AI scores every lead by conversion likelihood and drafts follow-up messages, so reps always know who to call next.

Close & Report

Drag leads across a visual pipeline to close. Managers see conversion rates and rep performance in real time.
