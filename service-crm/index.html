How It Works

From the order to the tenth year of service, on one record per machine.

Register

Every unit you install gets a record — customer, site, branch, model, serial, support type and contract dates. Import your existing base in one go.

Commission

Installation and commissioning runs through your own stage list, each with a due date counted from job start or from the stage before it.

Service

Preventive visits, breakdowns and warranty calls land in the service book, get assigned to an engineer, and close against the machine's history.

Renew

Units flag themselves when a visit is due or an AMC is near expiry — so renewals get chased while there's still a contract to renew.

What's In It

Installed-Base Register

Every machine with model, serial, fuel, support type, AMC window, visits used and next service due. Rows change colour the moment one needs attention.

The Service Book

Preventive maintenance, breakdowns and installation jobs in one log, each with its running sub-activity, owner and due date.

AMC & Warranty Tracking

Contract start and end, visits consumed against visits owed, overdue units and renewals due — counted per branch, not guessed.

Configurable Workflows

Build your own commissioning and FEMS stage lists with day-offset norms. Jobs already in flight keep the dates they were given.

Engineer Workload

Open jobs by technician, unassigned calls surfaced separately, and closures per period — so allocation is a decision, not a habit.

Branch-Scoped Roles

Admin, super user, regional manager and engineer. Managers run their own region end to end; engineers work their service book and read the rest.

Sales Orders

Open orders tracked alongside the service side, so a new installation arrives in the service book instead of being retyped into it.

Maintenance Alerts

A standing list of what is overdue, due soon or about to lapse — the first screen a coordinator opens in the morning.

Permissions & Settings

Role permissions editable in the app, branch and category masters under your control, and user management without a developer.
