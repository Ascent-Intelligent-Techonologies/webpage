How It Works

From raised to awarded, with a clock on every step and an owner on every request.

Intake

Requests come in through the web form or straight from your sourcing mailbox. Both land in the same queue with a reference number attached.

Route

Category and spend threshold decide the sourcing strategy, the minimum quotes, the target cycle and who has to approve it.

Work

A business-day SLA runs on every request — and pauses while you're waiting on the requester, so buyers aren't punished for someone else's delay.

Report

Pipeline, savings, cycle time, SLA breach rate and buyer workload update themselves. Export to Excel when finance asks.

What's In It

Email & Web Intake

Mails to your sourcing address become tracked requests. Keep the reference in the subject and every reply attaches itself to the right one.

Business-Day SLA Clocks

SLAs counted in working days, paused automatically while a request waits on the requester or sits on hold. Breaches surface before they're history.

Threshold-Based Routing

Spend bands drive the playbook — three quotes or a full RFP with e-auction, the target cycle, and the approver who signs it off.

AI Request Health

Every request scored on responsiveness, completeness, timeliness, sentiment and progress — with the risks named and the next step drafted.

Document Gates

Budget approval, SOW and BOQ are mandatory where they should be. Sourcing approval stays blocked until they're attached — no chasing.

Role-Aware Access

Requester, sourcing buyer, category focal, manager, admin and a read-only auditor — each sees their own scope, with delegation built in.

Spend Dashboards

Open pipeline by category, savings against requested value, cycle time by threshold band, aging, and buyer workload — all filterable.

Supplier & Policy Hub

Supplier directory, category policies, programmes and cross-functional contacts in the same place the request lives.

Full Audit Trail

Every assignment, status change, message and approval is logged and attributable — and exportable for internal audit.

A Closer Look

The request page is where the work actually happens — status, SLA, documents, people, and what to do next.
